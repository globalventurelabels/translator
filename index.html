Audio Setup (Important)

For conference calls: Enable Stereo Mix on your laptop so the app can hear both call audio and room speakers.
How to enable Stereo Mix (Windows):
  1. Right-click Volume icon (system tray) → Open Sound settings
  2. Scroll down → Advanced → Volume mixer
  3. Scroll down → Advanced → App volume and device preferences
  4. Find "Translation Bridge" → Set Output to "Speakers" and Input to "Stereo Mix"
  5. If Stereo Mix doesn't appear: Right-click speaker icon → Recording devices → Right-click empty space → Show disabled devices → Enable Stereo Mix
